Every card number on screen is masked, also sample numbers and ones the screen reading misread. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.10-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.10-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.9 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Card numbers by their look:**
  - Cuttle counted a number as a card only when its check digit and its first digits were a real card's. Sample numbers on a card's picture fail those checks. So does a real card when the screen reading misreads one digit.
  - On a page of card pictures, one of five numbers was masked.
  - Now a number written as a card's is masked as it is: four groups of digits (also with a digit too many or too few, as the screen reading slips), or American Express's 4 6 5.
  - A long number written otherwise, such as an order number, still needs the checks.
