Zooms that move like a camera: they glide into place, follow your pointer and blur as they move. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.11-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.11-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.10 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Zooms that settle like a camera:**
  - A zoom eased in and out over 0.6 s, and each of its moves stopped before the next. It also ended faster than it began.
  - Now it sets off at once and slows into place, as a camera on a spring would. It zooms as fast at its end as at its start.
- **A camera that follows your pointer:**
  - A zoom that followed your clicks moved about a second before each click. The pointer was often on its way up to two seconds earlier, out of view.
  - Now the view sets off as the pointer leaves the middle of the picture, in the direction it went. While the pointer stays well in view, the view holds still: no more small nods up and down.
- **Deeper, longer zooms:**
  - A window recorded on a Retina display got zooms of 1.1 to 1.3×, which looked like the picture slipping. Every zoom is now at least 1.5× deep.
  - Zooms on clicks and typing stay at least 2.4 s, over a second after the click, instead of going in and out within 1.9 s.
- **Motion blur:**
  - As a zoom moves, the screen blurs along its way, and the pointer along its path, as with a camera's shutter. It is light by default. Off, Film and Strong are in the Look tab, under Video.
  - Masks cover every place their text passes while the picture blurs, so nothing they hide smears out beside them.

New takes get the new zooms. Projects made before keep their zooms where they are, and those zooms now move and blur the new way.
