Fixes for the Mac: the editor's smooth playback, and the clicks and keys of a take. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.4-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.4-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1, 0.1.2 and 0.1.3 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Smooth playback of a Mac take:**
  - A Mac take has a frame only when the screen changes. ffmpeg read that as a million frames a second, and the smooth-playback copy failed after a still second ("No smooth-playback copy", ffmpeg's "Error muxing a packet").
  - Now the copy is made, and its frames lie exactly where the take's are. Before, even a copy that was made could be a few milliseconds off.
- **Clicks and keys on a Mac:**
  - Without Input Monitoring, recent macOS sends Cuttle no input at all, not even where the pointer goes. A take then had no clicks, no zooms on them, no typing and no smooth cursor, and Cuttle only warned about the keyboard.
  - Cuttle now asks for Input Monitoring when you pick what to record, not during the take. Without it, the picker says what the take misses and where to turn Cuttle on (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring, then quit and reopen Cuttle).
  - A take without it still keeps the pointer's way, so the cursor moves smoothly. The recording shows "No clicks or keys logged".
