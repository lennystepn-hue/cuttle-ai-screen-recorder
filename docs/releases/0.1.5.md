A full screen that stays whole, and a transcript you correct a phrase at a time. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.5-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.5-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.4 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **A full screen that stays whole:**
  - Cuttle crops a take to the page of the browser in front, without its tabs and bars. With no page in front, it should leave the take whole.
  - It missed the window that was in front when the take began. Unless another window came to the front, it then guessed a crop from the edges that hardly change: a Mac take lost 239 px on the left, part of the Dock and half the menu bar.
  - Now each take begins with what is in front. A full-screen take with no page in front stays whole, on Windows and on a Mac.
- **Correcting the transcript:**
  - The Transcript tab now says how: double-click a word, or select words (Shift+click) and choose *Edit text*.
  - Several words are corrected as one phrase, so a misheard "neues tun gebaut" becomes "neues Tool gebaut" in one go. The subtitles and the .srt and .vtt files follow.
  - *Undo fix* takes back the corrections in a selection.
- **Live captions while recording are off** until you switch them on (Settings, Speech). If you already switched them on, they stay on.
