Window takes on a Mac that keep their clicks, and a picker without Cuttle's own windows. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.6-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.6-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.5 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Window takes on a Mac keep their clicks:**
  - A window take logs only what you do in that window, and nothing while another window is in front.
  - While its window is recorded, the Claude app shows a tiny window of its own over its buttons. Without the Accessibility permission, Cuttle took that tiny window for the one in front. So it logged no click, no move and no key in the whole take, and set no zooms.
  - Now a small window of an app that lies inside one of its windows counts as part of it. A window take of the Claude app keeps its clicks and zooms, with or without Accessibility.
- **Cuttle's own windows stay out:** Cuttle's editor and settings no longer show among the windows to record. On a Mac, they no longer count as the window in front of a window take.
