Privacy masks that stay on to the last frame of what they hide, on a Mac too. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.7-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.7-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.6 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **No secret flashes as the screen changes:**
  - A Mac take has a frame only when the screen changes. Cuttle follows each private thing from frame to frame, but on such takes its frames came out a frame early.
  - So a mask ended one frame before the last frame that showed its secret. As a menu closed or the page went on, an email address or a key showed for one frame of the video.
  - Now each mask stays on to the last frame that shows its secret and comes on with the first. At most a frame of mask shows before and after.
