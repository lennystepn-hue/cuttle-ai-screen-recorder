Privacy masks that come on with what they hide, as a menu opens. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.8-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.8-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.7 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Masks that come on with what they hide:**
  - When a menu or a page opened with an email address or a key, Cuttle read it in the frame that showed it. But it took the moment of that reading a little early, a frame before the menu opened.
  - It then followed what was there before the menu (the text under it) and lost it at once. The address itself was masked only from the next reading, up to 5 s later. On one Mac take, 4 s of an account menu showed its email address.
  - Now each reading is followed from the frame it read. The mask comes on with that frame and stays as long as the address shows.
