Every time a menu opens with your email address, it is read and masked. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.1.9-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.1.9-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.8 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Every change on screen is read:**
  - Cuttle reads the screen's text where the picture changes, to find what it must mask. On a Mac take there is a frame only when the screen changes.
  - There, Cuttle could notice a change and then read the frame just before it. A menu that opened and closed again within a second was never read. On one take, an account menu showed its email address unmasked for 0.8 s.
  - Now Cuttle reads the very frame the change came in, and masks from there.
