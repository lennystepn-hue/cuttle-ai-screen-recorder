A big one: zoom paths, camera layouts, annotations, a narration and the computer's sound, 4K at 60 fps, transparent exports, subtitles in several languages, and Cuttle for any agent. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.2.0-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.2.0-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.1.13 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Zoom paths:** each move of a zoom picked, placed on the video, timed and eased.
- **Camera layouts:** for a while, the camera over the whole video, side by side with the screen, or as a large bubble. The bubble turns into the layout and back.
- **A narration:** lines of text spoken by a voice on this computer, or by one of your ElevenLabs voices. The computer's sound can be recorded beside the mic.
- **4K at 60 fps:** and HEVC where the graphics card encodes it, for smaller files.
- **Cuttle for any agent:** `cuttle mcp` serves Cuttle to Claude Desktop, Cursor or any MCP client, and `cuttle open` shows a project in the editor, which follows every change the agent makes.
- **Annotations:** arrows, boxes, ellipses, a highlighter, a spotlight and a magnifier.
- **Keystroke badges:** the shortcuts pressed in a take, as keycaps.
- **Text animations:** captions come in and go out with a pop, a slide, a blur, a typewriter, word by word or a sweep.
- **Several items at once:** select them on the timeline (*Ctrl*-click, *⌘*-click on a Mac, or a band dragged across them) and move, switch, duplicate or delete them together.
- **The music's stems:** drums, bass, keys, pads and lead, each at its own level in a mixer.
- **Loudness for where the video goes:** YouTube and the web (−14 LUFS), a podcast or broadcast.
- **Every format in one go:** 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16, the sound made once for all of them.
- **Transparent videos** for Premiere, Final Cut, Resolve or CapCut: WebM with alpha, or ProRes 4444.
- **Subtitles in several languages:** one in the picture, a file for each.
- **For your own code and CI:** Claude writes scene pages of its own (`cuttle motion add`) and `cuttle watch` checks each one as it is saved; `@cuttle/sdk` builds videos in TypeScript; the CLI renders on Linux and in Docker; and a GitHub Action turns a release's notes into a video. This release's video was made by it.
- **Fixed:** a running Cuttle, asked from outside to open a project, said the folder could not be opened.
