Screenshots, framed as the videos are. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.3.0-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.3.0-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.2.2 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **Screenshots:** *Ctrl+Shift+Alt+C* (*Ctrl+Shift+Option+C* on a Mac), the camera on the pill or *Screenshot* in the tray freezes the screen, so an open menu stays in the shot. Pick a window by itself, a display or a region, with a timer of 3, 5 or 10 s if you need one.
- **Framed as the videos are:** in a window, a browser or a phone, or just rounded with a shadow, on any of the video backgrounds or on nothing, pixel for pixel (a Retina screenshot keeps every pixel). It is copied at once, ready to paste.
- **The screenshot editor:** the look changes as you go. Copy the shot, save it as PNG, JPEG or WebP, or drag it straight into a chat. Every shot keeps a folder of its own in *Pictures/Cuttle*, listed beside the editor.
- **Ready to post:** sizes for Open Graph, X, Instagram, a story or the App Store; the shot set on an edge, or cut off by it; a headline in one of the videos' heading fonts; a watermark; your brand kit in one click; and a look kept as a style for the next shots.
- **Hide private data:** Cuttle reads the shot's text on your computer and covers email addresses, keys and tokens, card and phone numbers. Still look yourself before you share.
- **Screenshots in a take:** the HUD's camera (or the same keys) takes one of what you record. On the timeline it becomes a still in the video, or opens as a screenshot. Any frame of the video is an image too (*Ctrl+K*).
- **For agents:** `cuttle shot take` and `cuttle shot frame`, `cuttle render --still`, and the MCP tools `screenshot_take`, `screenshot_frame` and `screenshot_styles`.
- **Fixed:** on Windows the HUD and the pill were cut off at the edge, and their shadow looked boxy.
