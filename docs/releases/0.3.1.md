Cuttle at the top of your screen: the take in the menu bar, and the pill and the HUD out of the notch. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.3.1-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.3.1-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.3.0 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **The take in the menu bar:** while you record, a red dot and the take's time sit beside Cuttle's icon (two bars while paused, the step with a script); on Windows the tray icon turns red and its tooltip says the time. A click opens Stop, Pause, Mark slip, Marker, Screenshot and the steps.
- **Cuttle at the top edge:** *Where Cuttle waits* (Settings, General) can hide the pill until you rest the pointer on the middle of the screen's top edge. Then it comes out of the notch on a Mac that has one, from under the menu bar on one that has none, from the top edge on Windows, on a spring, and goes again when the pointer leaves. Passing over the edge, or dragging a window there, does not open it.
- **The HUD at the top edge:** *The HUD* (Settings, Recording) can make it a small island at the top centre while you record: beside the notch with the dot and the time, or a small pill under the menu bar. Rest the pointer on it for the whole HUD and the prompter. A marker, a slip or a screenshot shows in it for a moment.
- Both are off until you choose them: the pill and the HUD stay where they always were.
