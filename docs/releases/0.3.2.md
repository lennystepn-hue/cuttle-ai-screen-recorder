Cuttle lives at the top of your screen now. Cuttle records your screen, or Claude Code at work, and edits the take itself: it cuts the waiting, zooms on what matters, masks your secrets and frames it into a demo you can post. Everything runs on your computer.

## Download

- **Windows:** `Cuttle-0.3.2-win-x64.exe`. It installs for you alone, without admin rights, with Start menu and desktop shortcuts. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may not know it: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Cuttle-0.3.2-mac-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Cuttle into Applications. The first recording asks for the screen, the microphone, input monitoring and accessibility.

Cuttle also needs ffmpeg: `winget install Gyan.FFmpeg` on Windows, `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac. Its first start says what is missing.

Cuttle 0.1.1 to 0.3.1 update themselves to this version within a day, or at once with *Restart and update* (Settings, General).

## New in this version

- **At the top edge by default:** the pill waits out of sight until you rest the pointer on the middle of the screen's top edge, then comes out of the notch (or from under the menu bar, or from the top edge on Windows). While you record, the HUD is a small island there with the dot and the time; rest the pointer on it for the whole HUD.
- **Rather have them on screen?** *Where Cuttle waits* (Settings, General) and *The HUD* (Settings, Recording) bring the pill and the HUD back where they were. Your choice is kept.
